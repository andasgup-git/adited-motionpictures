ABOUT

ADited Motion Pictures is an Indian production house telling stories that travel. Our debut feature, দেবী চৌধুরানী, brought Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay's legendary heroine to the big screen as the first official Indo-UK co-production in Bengali cinema — released theatrically in India (September 2025) and now across the United States (September 4, 2026).

THE FILM

Devi Chowdhurani — The Bandit Queen of Bengal

The first woman freedom fighter of India against the British colonial rulers — a 134-minute Bengali epic with English subtitles.
Cast: Srabanti Chatterjee · Prosenjit Chatterjee · Sabyasachi Chakrabarty · Arjun Chakrabarty
Director: Subhrajit Mitra · Music: Pandit Bickram Ghosh
Based on Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay's classic novel · India release: September 2025 · US release: September 4, 2026
